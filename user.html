Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my shipment?
You can easily track your shipment by entering the Tracking ID provided in your confirmation email into the 'Track Package' box on the Overview page.
What if my cargo is damaged?
If you opted for Cargo Insurance during booking, open a support ticket under the 'Damaged Cargo' category. Please attach photos for a quick claim process.
Can I change the delivery address?
Yes, before the shipment reaches the destination country. Additional charges may apply based on the new distance.
